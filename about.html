What is MakeMe Top MBP?

MBP stands for MakeMe Top Business Profile. It is an online directory for travel businesses in Jammu & Kashmir, India.

Tourists use MBP to find and contact verified travel agencies, resorts, houseboats, and tour operators. Business owners use MBP to create a Google-indexed profile that generates direct enquiries.

Important — Please Read: MakeMe Top MBP is a listing directory only. We are not a travel agent, booking platform, tour operator, or tourist safety provider. We do not take responsibility for any experience, service, booking, or transaction involving businesses listed on this platform. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.

Govt Verified Badge

Some listings display a "Govt Verified" badge. This means the business submitted a government registration document (such as a J&K Tourism or FSSAI registration) at the time of listing.

The Govt Verified badge is informational only. MBP does not independently verify document authenticity or current validity. A badge does not mean MBP guarantees the business is safe, licensed, or trustworthy. Always confirm directly with the business before booking.

For Tourists

When you find a business through MBP, you contact and deal with that business directly. MBP is simply a directory that helped you find them.

  • Always verify business details independently before booking
  • Do not pay large sums in advance without proper receipts
  • Check recent reviews and ask for references if needed
  • Purchase travel insurance before visiting Kashmir
  • In emergencies, contact J&K Police (100) or Tourist Police (+91-194-2477540)
